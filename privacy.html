Privacy Policy

Pedometer Pro · Versions 1.0, 1.1 and 1.2

Version scope

The September 27, 2026 policy applies to version 1.0 for iPhone. The version 1.1 section covers the changes for that version, including the Pedometer Pro app and Daily steps widget on Apple Watch. The Usage analytics section covers version 1.2 and later.

Your activity is processed on your devices. The app requires no account and sends no Health or Motion activity data to the developer or an advertising service. Version 1.2 and later share anonymous usage statistics only if you choose Share.

Health and Motion access in version 1.0

With your permission, Pedometer Pro reads steps, walking and running distance, and flights climbed from Apple Health on iPhone. It uses Motion & Fitness data for live phone steps and available distance and floor information. Apple Health can include activity that Apple Watch records and syncs to your iPhone.

Activity is used to display today’s progress, history, goal results, streaks, badges, and celebrations. Apple Health access is read-only.

Local storage on iPhone

The app stores your goal, distance unit, earned badges, best streak, and celebration history in local app preferences. It also keeps a disposable display cache containing activity history, recent totals, refresh information, and values used to combine Health and Motion updates.

The display cache uses iOS file protection and is excluded from backups. iOS may clear it, and the app can rebuild it from the data you allow it to read. System backup and restore settings may affect app preferences separately.

Version 1.1

Active Energy and expanded step charts

Version 1.1 can read Apple Health’s Active Energy, shown as active calories, in addition to steps, walking and running distance, and flights climbed. It uses permitted Health data in Week, Month, and Year charts and to display activity and goal progress. The iPhone cache can include active calories and the Health and Motion values used for live step readings.

Goal plans and your paired Watch

The iPhone stores your dated goal plan, weekday rules, Today only adjustments, and the starting goal used to compare older activity. The app sends the current goal, goal plan, and distance unit to your paired Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service, along with identifiers and revisions used to recognize plan updates. The Watch app stores the received preferences locally. This preference exchange contains no Health records or Motion readings. Apple Health independently manages any activity synchronization you allow.

Watch activity and the Daily steps widget

On Apple Watch, the app reads the Health activity you allow and can use the Watch’s Motion & Fitness data to update steps while the app is open. It keeps protected local watchOS files for activity history and received preferences. These cache files are excluded from backups.

The Watch app shares one saved daily summary with its own Daily steps widget through a device-only Watch Keychain item. The summary can include the date and refresh status, steps, distance, stairs, active calories, goal, and distance unit. The Keychain item is available while the Watch is unlocked and is configured without iCloud Keychain synchronization. The widget uses the saved summary, and watchOS manages its refresh schedule.

Usage analytics (version 1.2 and later)

Pedometer Pro asks before collecting anything. If you tap Share, or turn on Settings → Share usage analytics, it sends anonymous usage statistics to our analytics provider, PostHog: the screens and buttons you use, app and device settings, Watch pairing, Health and Motion access status, device model, iOS and app version, language, time zone, crash reports, and a random ID for this install. They never include your Health or Motion & Fitness data, aren’t linked to your identity, and aren’t used for advertising or tracking. We use them to fix crashes and improve the app. PostHog processes them for us and provides the same or equal protection described in this policy. We keep them for about one year. You can turn sharing off anytime in Settings, and the app stops sending them.

Advertising, tracking, and system services

Pedometer Pro has no ads, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated activity server. Version 1.2 and later use PostHog only for the anonymous usage analytics described above. The developer receives no activity history from the app.

Apple Health storage and synchronization, App Store services, and system diagnostics are governed by Apple’s controls and privacy practices. Apple’s TestFlight service handles beta feedback and diagnostics for TestFlight users.

Your choices and deletion

Review Pedometer Pro’s read access in Apple Health or your device’s Health privacy settings. Manage Motion & Fitness in your device’s privacy settings where available. Health and Motion permissions are independent. Revoking either permission stops new access through that source. A display cache can still show a saved reading.

Choose Delete App to remove ordinary local app files and preferences. To manage the underlying activity records, use Apple Health. Reinstalling and granting permission can reread records still available there.

For version 1.1, remove Daily steps from your Smart Stack or watch face to remove it from display. watchOS manages retention of the separate device-only widget Keychain item. The Watch app replaces that item when it publishes a newer daily summary. Contact support with questions about the saved Watch summary. The developer has no activity account or server-held activity history.

For version 1.2 and later, turn usage analytics off anytime in Settings → Share usage analytics.

If you contact support

If you email goodmeatsoftware@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. This is separate from the app’s automatic operation. We use correspondence to respond to requests, investigate problems, and meet applicable obligations. Email service providers process these messages.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed for these purposes and applicable legal obligations. You can request access to or deletion of your correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to applicable retention requirements. Please do not send passwords or full Health exports.

This support website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies to these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website information. Visiting these pages does not give GitHub access to your Health data through Pedometer Pro.

Children

Pedometer Pro is a general fitness utility, not a child-directed service. It does not ask users for their age or require a profile. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or support practices change. The current policy and effective date will be available on this page. We will post the effective date for the version 1.1 policy when that update becomes available. The Usage analytics section applies from version 1.2. New data access that requires permission begins after you grant it.
